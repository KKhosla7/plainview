Open local files

Use Ctrl+O / Cmd+O, or drag a file into the browser. OS file associations are managed by your operating system, not the extension.

Supported: .md, .json, .jsonc, .swagger, .openapi, .yaml, .toml, .csv, .tsv, .xml, .log, .rss and .atom.
